FICTIONAL EVALUATION DOCUMENT — Nova Digital Transport Policy
Version 1

1. Policy overview
This fictional policy covers domestic employee commuting at Nova Digital. It applies from 1 January 2026 until replaced through controlled activation. Transport supports attendance on approved shifts and is not a cash benefit.

Operational procedure: The policy owner is the local HR and administration team. A published replacement is authoritative only after successful activation in the policy system. Employees should use the currently active text rather than screenshots of old answers. The program does not promise availability on routes that have not been published. Route notices provide operational schedules and cannot waive approvals, safety duties or cost limits in this policy.

2. Employee eligibility
Full-time employees and registered interns with current HR records may enroll. Agency contractors are excluded unless HR grants a time-limited exception. Employees must complete the safety briefing before the first trip.

Operational procedure: Submit enrollment through the employee portal using the current employee identifier. HR validates employment status and sends an eligibility confirmation to the desk. Report transfers, changes in employment status and extended leave promptly. A colleague may not lend an enrollment or travel using another employee’s identity. The desk may pause a booking while records are inconsistent and must explain which record needs correction.

3. Grade requirements
Grades N1 through N6 are eligible for pooled cabs. A dedicated vehicle is permitted only for approved accessibility needs, irrespective of grade. Seniority does not automatically change the approved travel category.

Operational procedure: HR maintains the authoritative grade register. A grade change takes effect for transport after the updated record is verified; informal promotion announcements are insufficient. Employees requesting an exception must provide the approval reference rather than changing their declared grade. Grade eligibility does not replace shift, distance or cutoff requirements. The desk records any temporary category assignment and its expiry for subsequent audit.

4. Distance eligibility
The registered residence must be at least 3 km and no more than 40 km from the assigned office on the approved road route. HR and Admin must jointly approve addresses outside these limits.

Operational procedure: The desk calculates distance using the approved road-route tool and stores the route reference with enrollment. Straight-line map distance and personal estimates are not authoritative. An address change requires a new distance check before the first pickup at the new location. Employees should not disclose colleagues’ addresses in support requests. Changes to a safe stop are coordinated centrally and do not independently alter the approved service radius.5. Shift eligibility
Shifts ending at or after 10 PM or starting before 6 AM qualify for night transport. Other approved shifts may use an available pooled route. The supervisor must enter the shift in the roster before the cutoff.

Operational procedure: The supervisor must enter a shift change in the scheduling system before the booking deadline. Employees should include their shift reference with any eligibility query. A clock time alone cannot establish personal eligibility without the applicable roster, employment and distance records. The desk resolves discrepancies with the supervisor. Repeated changes made only to obtain transport are reviewed by HR rather than automatically approved by dispatch.

6. Pickup and drop arrangements
Employees use designated pickup hubs and should arrive 10 minutes early. Vehicles wait at most 5 minutes. Night drops use the registered doorstep when road access and driver safety allow it; the desk confirms alternatives.

Operational procedure: Track the assigned vehicle using the portal when available. If the vehicle or driver differs from the booking, verify the change with the desk before boarding. Do not share live location or driver contact details publicly. Route deviations must be reported to dispatch and recorded with the operational reason. Drivers may decline unsafe stops; the desk must coordinate a safe alternative and communicate it to affected travelers.

7. Night transportation
A 9:30 PM shift end does not qualify for the night-transport category. Such employees may use an approved pooled route if one is available. A special late trip requires the ad-hoc authorization in section 11.

Operational procedure: Employees must verify the vehicle identifier and driver identity before boarding. A delayed employee should contact dispatch rather than request unsafe driving or informal roadside pickup. The desk tracks completion of night drops and escalates an unconfirmed arrival. Report a route or safety concern immediately. The approved booking record, actual dispatch time and incident reference are retained for review by authorized operations personnel.

8. Security escorts
A trained security escort is mandatory for female employees on drops departing after 10 PM and before 5 AM. At exactly 10 PM this requirement does not yet apply. The desk assigns the escort and confirms safe completion of each escorted trip.

Operational procedure: The desk verifies escort assignment against the actual departure time and the applicable safety rule. A driver alone is not treated as a separately assigned security escort. If an assigned escort is absent, contact the desk and wait for a safe resolution. Operational delays cannot waive the rule. Report escort conduct concerns through the same incident channel; the duty lead coordinates a safe replacement and records the action.9. Weekend transportation
Saturday and Sunday cab booking is available to eligible employees on rostered work shifts without separate weekend approval. The shift must already appear in the approved duty roster and normal booking cutoffs still apply.

Operational procedure: Weekend requests must identify the date, shift, office and business purpose. Capacity remains subject to route planning and applicable cutoffs. A weekend service does not authorize personal errands, shopping or recreation. Keep the approval or roster reference available for dispatch verification. If work is cancelled, cancel the booking through the normal procedure. A holiday overlapping a weekend follows the separate holiday rule rather than whichever rule appears more convenient.

10. Holiday transportation
Public-holiday transport is available only for an approved essential-services roster with Admin approval. A weekend roster does not automatically qualify as a holiday roster. The published office holiday calendar determines the category.

Operational procedure: Admin publishes the office holiday calendar and identifies the duty roster used for dispatch. An employee should confirm the local office calendar when sites observe different holidays. A manager’s general work message is not a substitute for the required holiday authorization. Submit corrections to Admin before the booking deadline. Safety requirements, employee eligibility, cost restrictions and cancellation obligations continue to apply to every approved holiday trip.

11. Ad-hoc requests
Ad-hoc business trips require the project director and Admin to approve the request before dispatch. Provide an itinerary and business cost centre. An available vehicle is not evidence of authorization.

Operational procedure: Use the portal request form and attach the written approval reference before dispatch. The desk records route feasibility, estimated fare and the requesting cost centre. Approval of a business purpose does not override a fare cap or required security escort. Notify the approver of material itinerary changes. The final booking record must identify the actual traveler and destination rather than a generic team label that prevents accountability.

12. Airport transportation
Airport trips require project-director approval and Finance confirmation of the travel budget before booking. They must support a business itinerary. Personal airport transfers and companion tickets are outside this commuting policy.

Operational procedure: Provide flight number, terminal, pickup address and scheduled departure or arrival time. The desk allows a practical airport reporting buffer but cannot guarantee airline check-in. Notify dispatch of flight changes as soon as known. Additional waiting charges count toward the applicable cost limit. An airport approval applies only to the submitted itinerary; a changed business itinerary must be reconfirmed by the authorized approver before dispatch.13. Guest transportation
Guest transportation is not provided under the employee commuting program. Hosts must arrange approved vendor travel through the separate visitor-services process. A guest cannot be added to an employee roster to bypass this restriction.

Operational procedure: The sponsoring employee coordinates visitor identity verification through the approved visitor channel. Do not put passport numbers or other sensitive identity documents in ordinary chat questions. The desk checks the authorized travel category before assigning a seat. Where this policy excludes a visitor category, a host must use the separate visitor process rather than relabel the visitor as an employee. Approved passengers remain subject to vehicle safety capacity.

14. Cost restrictions
The maximum approved cab cost is INR 1800 per trip, including tolls and parking. Finance and Admin must jointly approve an over-limit quotation before dispatch. Unapproved excess is not automatically reimbursable.

Operational procedure: The desk obtains a fare estimate before confirming the booking and records all required financial approval references. Include expected tolls, parking and waiting charges in the estimate. Report an unexpected overrun promptly for review; post-trip review is not a promise of reimbursement. Employees must not pay an unofficial cash supplement to bypass the approved process. Finance reconciles the final vendor invoice against the booking and authorized exceptions.

15. Booking cut-off timings
Book regular trips by 6 PM on the preceding working day. Airport requests require 48 hours notice. Missed cutoffs are handled as ad-hoc requests and may be declined if approvals or capacity are unavailable.

Operational procedure: All cutoff times refer to the assigned office’s local time. A preceding working day excludes days marked closed in the office calendar. Submit early when a long holiday closure affects planning. The portal timestamp is the record of submission, and a draft saved without submission is not a booking. Late requests remain unconfirmed until the required approval and capacity checks are complete; do not assume a vehicle will arrive.

16. Cancellation procedures
Cancel through the portal at least 1 hour before pickup. Later cancellations normally count as no-shows. Report provider delays or verified medical emergencies to the desk so that the attendance record can be reviewed.

Operational procedure: A portal cancellation should produce a confirmation reference. If the portal is unavailable, notify the desk using the published contact and retain the incident reference. Do not rely on a message to a colleague or driver as the only cancellation record. The desk reviews disputed late cancellations using timestamps and the reported circumstances. Cancelled work shifts do not automatically cancel employee-created ad-hoc bookings, so check each reservation.17. No-show penalties
The first two no-shows in a calendar month receive reminders without a charge. From the third no-show, an INR 300 charge applies to each incident after HR review and employee notification. Documented operator failures are excluded.

Operational procedure: A no-show means the employee did not board the confirmed service and did not cancel within the permitted window. The desk records dispatch and wait timestamps before assigning the incident. Employees may dispute an incorrect record through the transport desk with available evidence. HR reviews contested penalties and any applicable exception. A driver’s failure to reach the approved pickup location must be investigated rather than treated as an employee absence.

18. Approval hierarchy
Project directors approve airport and ad-hoc business purpose. Finance confirms airport budgets and excess costs; Admin controls roster and dispatch approval. Reporting managers cannot independently approve an airport trip.

Operational procedure: Approvals must be attributable, dated and specific to the trip or valid exception period. The desk records the approval reference and checks that the approver holds the required role. Delegation must be documented by HR or Admin; a copied colleague cannot create approval authority. If multiple approvals are required, obtain all of them before dispatch. Conflicting approvals are escalated to the policy owner, with no assumption that a later informal message supersedes policy.

19. Exceptions
Distance or eligibility exceptions require joint HR and Admin approval for a stated period. A safety emergency must be reported immediately to the duty desk. A retrospective operational record is required within one working day.

Operational procedure: An exception records the reason, scope, approvers, start date and expiry date. It cannot override an immediate vehicle safety requirement or legal road restriction. The desk checks the exception on each affected booking and removes expired exceptions from operational use. Employees should request renewal before expiry. A previous approved exception for a different traveler, location or month does not establish a continuing entitlement for a new request.

20. Transport desk and escalation
Contact mobility@nova.example or extension 5202 for transport support. Escalate unresolved dispatch issues to the Admin duty manager, then the people-operations director. In imminent danger use local emergency services first.

Operational procedure: Provide the booking reference, relevant trip time and a concise description when contacting support. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or unrelated personal documents. The desk acknowledges the incident and routes it to the duty owner; urgent safety reports take priority over routine booking changes. Keep the incident reference for follow-up. Feedback on an assistant answer helps review content but does not amend policy or create a booking approval.
